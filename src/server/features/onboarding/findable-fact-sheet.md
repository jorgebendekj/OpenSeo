## What Findable is

Findable is a commercial, globally available SaaS platform for SEO and AI-search visibility, operated by Ribentek AI.

Findable answers the core modern search question: **Find out whether AI and search engines recommend your business. Pay for what you use, never for seats.**

It unifies AI engine visibility (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews) alongside traditional search intelligence (keyword research, domain analytics, backlinks, rank tracking, site audits, and Google Search Console).

Findable is AI-native, exposing a high-performance MCP server so agents (Claude, Cursor, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Codex) can interact with your search visibility data securely.

## Positioning and Philosophy

1. **AI Visibility + SEO Unified**: Modern discovery happens across search engines and generative AI answers. Findable tracks both in one place.
2. **Pay-as-you-go, Never per Seat**: Teams are never charged for adding teammates or clients. You only pay for search data queries and computation actually used.
3. **Transparent Credit Ledger**: All credit transactions are recorded in an append-only ledger. Failed requests are never charged and are immediately refunded. Viewing, sorting, filtering, and re-reading cached data is free forever.

## Pricing and Credits

- **Trial Grant**: 500 complimentary credits on signup with no credit card required.
- **Active User Monthly Allowance**: 100 credits/month for accounts with at least one login in the last 30 days.
- **Credit Purchases**: One-time top-ups via Stripe starting at $25 (available in USD, EUR, GBP, PLN) with automated Stripe Tax compliance.
- **Volume Bonuses**:
  - +10% extra credits on 12-month cumulative spend over $100.
  - +20% extra credits on 12-month cumulative spend over $500.
- **Auto-Topup**: Opt-in automatic balance replenishment when credits drop below a custom threshold.
- **Free Actions**: Browsing, filtering, viewing historical reports, data exports, and cached analyses cost 0 credits.
- **Runway & Grace Period**: Findable projects daily burn rate and alerts users at 14, 7, and 2 days before exhaustion. A 7-day grace period ensures rank tracking history is preserved.

## Core Workflows

- **AI Search Visibility**: Check brand and product citations across leading LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) and track AI recommendation market share against competitors.
- **Keyword Research**: Search volume, keyword difficulty, search intent, cost-per-click (CPC), and multi-year trend patterns.
- **Domain Overview**: Organic footprint, ranking keywords, traffic estimates, and competitor gap comparisons for any domain globally.
- **Backlink Intelligence**: Referring domains, anchor distributions, backlink quality attributes, and link velocity.
- **Rank Tracking**: Automated daily/weekly rank tracking across desktop and mobile devices with customizable SERP depth.
- **Technical Site Audits**: Comprehensive crawler auditing indexability, status codes, metadata, headings, page speed metrics, and accessibility signals.
- **Google Search Console**: Free integration importing real user clicks, impressions, average positions, and URL inspection data.

## Multi-Tenancy and Teams

- **Multi-Tenant Isolation**: Organizations can manage multiple client projects with complete data isolation.
- **Roles**: Owner, Admin, Member, and read-only Client.
- **Unlimited Seats**: Invite team members and clients without seat caps or per-user subscription fees.
