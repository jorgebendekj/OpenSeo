# GEO playbook: becoming the cited answer in Spain

Nobody can guarantee a position in ChatGPT or AI Overviews. The plan raises the probability by being the clearest, most corroborated source on a narrow topic.

## Target entity

"Findable: herramienta de SEO y visibilidad en IA que se paga por uso, sin cuota por usuario." Use the same sentence in the site, G2, Capterra, Product Hunt, LinkedIn, Crunchbase and every directory.

## Workstreams

### A. On-site (done / ongoing)
Spanish home, five guides, schema, `llms.txt`, hreflang. Next: pricing and feature pages in Spanish, author pages with real names, update dates.

### B. Off-site corroboration (the slow part)
1. Listings: G2, Capterra, GetApp, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, Crunchbase, general AI-tool directories and Spanish marketing directories (research which ones rank for the target queries).
2. Be present in the roundups AI engines cite. Search the target questions, list the cited URLs, and pitch each author with a factual summary and a free test account. Track in a sheet.
3. One original data piece (see 03, item 9): publish methodology and raw table; this is the asset others link to.
4. Two partner placements: Spanish SEO/marketing newsletters, communities and meetups (shortlist after the roundup research), offering a webinar on "medir GEO sin pagar 100 USD al mes".
5. Reviews: ask early users for honest G2/Capterra reviews. Never buy or write them.

### C. Distribution
Spanish LinkedIn posts from a named founder, YouTube walkthrough in Spanish (screen recording of the manual method), Spanish-language SEO communities and X/Twitter.

### D. Product (unlocks the claim)
Country and language selector on Brand Lookup (Spain 2724 / es). Then publish the first Spain data study with Findable's own numbers.

## Measurement routine (monthly)

1. 30 Spanish prompts in four types (discovery, comparison, local, brand) run in ChatGPT and Google España; log appear/position/sources/errors.
2. Search Console: impressions and clicks for `/es` and `/blogs/*` pages with `lang: es`; non-brand query count.
3. GA4: sessions with source chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com.
4. Signups from Spain (country in app analytics).
5. Share of voice vs competitor set (Brand Lookup, with the US caveat noted).

## 90-day plan

| Weeks | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Ship branch, submit `/es` and sitemap in Search Console, baseline measurement, 10 customer calls | Baseline sheet |
| 3-4 | Listings and entity consistency; 2 new guides (ecommerce, local) | 8 listings live |
| 5-8 | Data study fieldwork and publication; pitch 20 roundup authors | Study + 5 mentions |
| 9-10 | Spanish pricing/feature pages; Brand Lookup Spain selector | Product claim true |
| 11-12 | Review, remeasure, double down on winning cluster | Month-3 report |

Success at day 90 (targets, not promises): indexed `/es` and all guides; ≥ 20 non-brand queries with impressions in Search Console; Findable named in at least 3 of the 30 tracked prompts; ≥ 5 independent Spanish-language mentions.
