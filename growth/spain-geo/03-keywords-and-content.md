## Page standards for every Spanish piece

- Direct answer in the first paragraph; title under 60 characters where possible.
- One primary keyword, 2-3 variants, an FAQ block with visible Q&A.
- Honest product framing: say what Findable does not do (no Spain-specific ChatGPT data yet).
- Internal links: guide ↔ guide, one link to `/es`, one to a feature page.
- `lang: "es"` frontmatter, dated, updated when facts change.
- Spanish from Spain (vosotros not needed, but "ordenador", "autónomo", "IVA"), not neutral-LatAm.
